# 💖 Support & Sponsor Synapse Shield

**Synapse Shield** is an open-source, privacy-first behavioral biometrics and bot mitigation engine designed as a zero-cost, self-hosted, zero-PII alternative to intrusive CAPTCHAs and expensive proprietary cloud WAFs.

Built and actively maintained by **Mustafa Güngör ([@0xStoic-bit](https://github.com/0xStoic-bit))** alongside community contributors.

---

## 🎯 Why Sponsor?

Synapse Shield is **100% free, MIT-licensed open-source software**. 

Your sponsorship directly funds:
- 🧪 **Continuous Security Audits & Red Team Testing**: Hardware testing across mobile devices (iOS, Android, tablets) and automated anti-bot bypass research.
- ⚡ **Infrastructure & CI/CD Runners**: Cross-compilation matrix runners for universal Python/Rust `abi3` wheels across Linux (`x86_64`, `aarch64`, `musl`), macOS (`Apple Silicon`, `Intel`), and Windows.
- 📚 **Documentation & Framework Integrations**: Maintaining zero-dependency adapters across FastAPI, Litestar, Django, Flask, Sanic, and modern frontend SDKs (Vanilla JS, React, Vue).
- 🛡️ **Zero-PII Privacy Research**: Advancing non-invasive mathematical biometric modeling (jerk kinematics, micro-tremor modeling, Fitts's law terminal deceleration) without tracking users or harvesting cookies.
